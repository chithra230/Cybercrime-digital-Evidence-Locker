About The System

The Cybercrime Digital Evidence Locker is designed to provide a secure platform for managing digital evidence related to cybercrime complaints.

The system helps maintain evidence integrity, secure access, complaint tracking and organized evidence management.

Digital evidence such as screenshots, transaction receipts, URLs, phone numbers and other relevant digital files can be managed through the system.
